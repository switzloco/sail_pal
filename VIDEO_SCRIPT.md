# Vessel Ops AI - Gemma 4 Good Hackathon Video Script

**Target Length:** 2:30 - 3:00 minutes
**Actor/Speaker:** Capt. Chris Oprzadek
**Setting:** Ship's bridge or Engine Room (or a simulated environment resembling one), followed by screen recordings of the app.

---

**[0:00 - 0:30] INTRO & THE PROBLEM**
*(Camera opens on Capt. Chris Oprzadek looking out a window or standing near nautical equipment.)*

**Capt. Chris Oprzadek:**
"When you're two hundred miles offshore, isolation isn't just a feeling—it's an operational reality. Out here, there's no high-speed internet. If a piece of critical machinery breaks down, we can't just Google a solution. And if a crew member gets seriously injured, there's no doctor to call for a second opinion. We rely entirely on the manuals we carry and our own training. But what if we could bring the world's most advanced intelligence right to the edge—with zero connectivity required?"

**[0:30 - 1:15] THE SOLUTION**
*(Cut to Chris sitting at a desk with a standard, mid-range laptop. He opens Vessel Ops AI.)*

**Capt. Chris Oprzadek (Voiceover):**
"Enter Vessel Ops AI. We built this for the Gemma 4 Good Hackathon to solve the very real problem of maritime isolation. It's an offline-first assistant powered by Gemma 4, running entirely locally on standard ship hardware using Ollama."

*(Screen recording showing the clean, intuitive interface of Vessel Ops AI. The "Offline Mode" indicator is clearly visible.)*

**Capt. Chris Oprzadek (Voiceover):**
"It requires no technical skills to install. Just one click, and you have a frontier AI model ready to assist the Medical Person In Charge or the Chief Engineer."

**[1:15 - 2:00] DEMO: MEDICAL RAG & MULTIMODAL**
*(Screen recording showing the Health Log feature.)*

**Capt. Chris Oprzadek (Voiceover):**
"Let's look at a medical scenario. A crew member suffers a deep laceration. The Medical Person In Charge needs immediate guidance. Because we've integrated Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Gemma 4 isn't guessing. It's actively retrieving protocols from the embedded World Health Organization's International Medical Guide for Ships."

*(Show the AI generating a response, explicitly citing WHO IMGS guidelines.)*

**Capt. Chris Oprzadek (Voiceover):**
"And with Gemma 4's multimodal capabilities, we can even upload a photo of the injury or a broken engine part, and the AI helps us log, triage, and diagnose the issue right on the spot."

**[2:00 - 2:30] OFFLINE SYNC & ENGINEERING**
*(Screen recording showing the Maintenance Log and Sync Status.)*

**Capt. Chris Oprzadek (Voiceover):**
"Every action, every log, and every piece of advice is saved locally using our robust SQLite backend. When the vessel finally reaches port and regains an internet connection, our offline-first queuing system automatically syncs everything securely to Firebase, keeping shore-side management in the loop without missing a beat."

**[2:30 - 3:00] CONCLUSION**
*(Cut back to Capt. Chris on camera.)*

**Capt. Chris Oprzadek:**
"Vessel Ops AI isn't just an app; it's a lifeline. By taking Google's Gemma 4 and pushing it to the extreme edge, we're empowering crews to operate safer, smarter, and with greater resilience. We're bringing the future of AI to the places that need it the most. Thank you."

*(Fade to black. Title card: "Vessel Ops AI - Powered by Gemma 4. Github link.")*
