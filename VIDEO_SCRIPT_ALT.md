# Vessel Ops AI - Gemma 4 Good Hackathon Video Script (Chill/Credibility Version)

**Target Length:** 2:00 - 2:30 minutes
**Actor/Speaker:** Capt. Chris Oprzadek
**Setting:** Casual but professional. Sitting at a desk, maybe a boat cabin or a relaxed home office with some nautical/technical gear in the background. Screen recordings of the app intercut throughout.

---

**[0:00 - 0:45] INTRO & CREDIBILITY**
*(Camera on Chris. Relaxed, conversational tone.)*

**Capt. Chris Oprzadek:**
"Hey everyone, I'm Chris Oprzadek. To give you a quick background on where I'm coming from—I spent about eight years in the Navy as a Nuclear Submarine Officer, deploying out of Hawaii and Guam. When you're operating underwater, you learn very quickly what it means to be completely cut off from the outside world.

After the Navy, I spent almost a decade at Stryker in business transformation and global sourcing, which is actually where I met Nick. Nowadays, I'm an RYA Cruising Instructor and a maritime engineer. So, I spend a lot of time on boats, far offshore."

**[0:45 - 1:20] THE "AHA" MOMENT**
**Capt. Chris Oprzadek:**
"When you're out at sea, isolation is the biggest risk factor. If an engine fails, or if a crew member gets seriously hurt, you can't just Google a solution. You rely on your training and the physical manuals you have on board. 

So when Nick called me up and said, 'Hey, what if we could put a frontier AI model completely offline, running locally on a standard laptop?'... my mind was blown. He showed me Google's Gemma 4 running through Ollama. No cloud. No Starlink required. Just the laptop."

**[1:20 - 2:00] THE APP DEMO & RAG**
*(Cut to screen recording of the app: User interface, showing the "Offline Mode" indicator, interacting with the Medical chat.)*

**Capt. Chris Oprzadek (Voiceover):**
"That's why we built Vessel Ops AI. We took the World Health Organization's *International Medical Guide for Ships* and embedded it directly into the system using RAG. So if you have a medical emergency, you're getting grounded, textbook-backed advice instantly. 

*(Cut to screen recording of the Maintenance/Component upload.)*
And because Gemma 4 is multimodal, you can snap a picture of a sheared bolt in the engine room, and the AI helps you troubleshoot it right on the spot."

**[2:00 - 2:30] CONCLUSION**
*(Cut back to Chris on camera.)*

**Capt. Chris Oprzadek:**
"For the first time, the Medical Person in Charge or the Chief Engineer has a reliable second opinion out on the water. And when you finally hit port, the system automatically syncs all your logs to the cloud. 

We're taking Gemma 4 and pushing it to the absolute edge of the map. Thanks for checking out Vessel Ops AI."
